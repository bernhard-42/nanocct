# 2d. What is not bound, and the workaround

Part of the design documents in `docs/`, which share their section numbers: 6.1–6.6, 6a–6c and every `R-…` rule identifier are [Binding-Rules.md](Binding-Rules.md), all other numbers (2a, 4.2, 8a, …) are [Design.md](Design.md).

Members that nanocct cannot bind and that a user might look for, with the OCCT-level alternative. The complete list of omissions is `src/cpp/<TK>/report.txt` per toolkit (categories in 8a); everything internal to an algorithm is left out of this table on purpose. **Maintained per toolkit: every new toolkit's report is checked for user-facing omissions and this table extended.**
